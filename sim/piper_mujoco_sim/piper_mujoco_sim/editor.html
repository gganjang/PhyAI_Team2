Frames and rotation

The mount is the parent of the arm camera. Moving or rotating the mount carries its pad, housing, and lens together. Camera adjustments are relative to the mount.

Position is in centimeters. Rotation uses roll about X, pitch about Y, then yaw about Z, in the parent frame. Saved rotations use normalized quaternions. Image roll affects the view independently.
